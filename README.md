# BookHUD 

**A Minecraft HUD Mod for reading books while walking.**

BookHUD is a simple HUD Utility Mod that projects pages of a book on the right upper corner of your screen while outside of any menus, as long as you have a book in your offhand. 

Originally intended for reading recipes and guidebooks as you play, without having to open and close the book over and over again.

## Mod Features 

An overview of the mod's current and planned features.

### Current Features 

- In-world book HUD
- Turn pages with rebindable keybinds
  - `[` and `]` by default
- Equip a Compatible Item on offhand to activate
- Compatible Items 
  - Written Book
